    Available live inputs

    GPS provides position, accuracy, heading, and sometimes receiver speed. DeviceMotion may provide raw acceleration and rotation. OBD-II and camera/ADAS require an external connected device.

    Persistence

    Raw sensor payloads, source, observed time, server receipt time, and derived GPS speed provenance are stored in SQLite. The crash-severity model and evaluation summary are saved separately under models/.

    Device API

    Post raw readings to /api/sensors/gps, /api/sensors/motion, /api/sensors/obd, or /api/sensors/vision.

    Deployment boundary

    The historical severity classifier is not a live collision-prediction model, and no emergency-service connection exists. Secure device authentication, encrypted transport, independently validated models, and approved dispatch integration are required before production.
